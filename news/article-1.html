Bearmentor Graduation: From Zero to Fullstack in 6 Months

Today, I am thrilled to share a milestone that means a great deal to me. After more than six months of intensive learning at the Bearmentor bootcamp, I have officially completed the Fullstack Web Development program. To be honest, reaching this point felt like a distant dream. Starting with zero experience in web development was a significant challenge, but having the right guidance made everything possible.

I would like to express my deepest gratitude to my incredible mentors: Bang M Haidar Hanif, Bang Ben Nata, and Bang Raditya Dwisatrio Santoso. Thank you for your patience in guiding me and for providing such a solid foundation.

Congratulations also to my peers in Batch 8 and Batch 9, especially Bang Endi Suwandi, Bang Ammar Ismail Khocan, and Bang Akhirudin Salasa. It has been a privilege to know and collaborate with all of you, especially as we worked side-by-side to bring our final project, acaraga.com, to life. Our teamwork was truly one of the highlights of this journey.

I realize that graduation is not the finish line — it's where the real journey begins.

I now feel ready and equipped with a solid foothold to forge my path in the tech industry. Onwards and upwards! 🚀

Purnomo Arif

Fullstack Web Developer
